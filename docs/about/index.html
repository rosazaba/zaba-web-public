The person behind the lens

Hi! I'm Rosa

Walking barefoot, salt-kissed and close to the sea as a way of life. That this is my work uniform is crazy. My great passions are the sea, photography and sport. Being able to bring them all together and turn them into a profession, coming from Toledo, hasn't been luck.

I'm a photographer, filmmaker and founder of my own brand. Based in Fuerteventura, I have spent more than eight years in the diving industry and more than three as a professional photographer, making my way in the surf world while I keep learning and evolving.

More

The ocean taught me to slow down in a world that rushes, to wait for the right moment to press the shutter and bring to life an image my mind had already imagined. My office has no walls or ceiling, because my mind didn't have them either when it came to setting limits of time or age. And you? Are you who you want to be? All it takes is the will — no hurry.
