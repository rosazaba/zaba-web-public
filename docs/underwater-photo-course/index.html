Underwater photography

Underwater photo course

Learn to take your own photos underwater: a hands-on course with theory, practice in the water and editing, in small groups and at your own pace.

01

Theory

Light underwater, camera settings, composition and how to move without disturbing marine life. Tell me your starting point and we'll build from there, adapting the course to your interests.

02

Practice in the water

We go into the water together and put everything into practice, step by step. On the first dive we'll do some basic exercises to get comfortable with manual mode and light, and on the second dive you'll be able to make your own decisions and develop a more artistic side. It's very important to know your level and experience underwater too: good buoyancy is essential, out of respect for marine life and for safety.

03

Editing

We choose the best photos, try out different ways of organising them and edit them together in different versions, so you can find your personal style. There's no single right way to do it. Think about what you want to convey and what your goal is with each photo.
